Privacy Policy for VibeMatch! Blind Date

Last updated: September 26, 2026

VibeMatch! Blind Date ("VibeMatch", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the VibeMatch! Blind Date mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use VibeMatch, we may collect:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address associated with your account
  • Profile information such as age, qualification, profession, interests, and preferences
  • Profile photographs that you choose to upload
  • Likes, matches, messages, and other interactions within the application
  • Images that you choose to send through chat
  • Information required to provide mood-based matching features
  • Device and technical information required for app operation, security, analytics, and advertising

2. Camera and Mood Detection

VibeMatch may request access to your device camera when you choose to use mood detection or mood scanning features.

Camera access is used to analyze facial expressions for the purpose of providing the mood-related feature. You control whether the application receives camera permission through your device settings.

Camera access is not required to use every feature of VibeMatch.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Create and display your profile
  • Provide matching and discovery features
  • Provide mood-based matching functionality
  • Enable likes, matches, and messaging
  • Provide image sharing and profile photo functionality
  • Maintain application security and prevent abuse
  • Improve application performance and functionality
  • Display advertisements
  • Respond to support requests

4. Firebase and Google Services

VibeMatch uses Firebase and other Google services to provide application functionality. These services may process information such as authentication information, application data, device information, and technical information as necessary to provide their services.

VibeMatch uses Firebase Authentication for account authentication and Cloud Firestore for application data such as profiles, interactions, notifications, and messages.

5. Advertising

VibeMatch may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising services may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app usage information, and other information permitted by applicable settings and laws.

You may have controls available through your Android device and Google account for managing advertising personalization and related settings.
